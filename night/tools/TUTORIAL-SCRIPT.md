# Deep Drift tutorial: narration script

Record each line as its **own file**, named exactly as in the **File** column
(`intro.wav`, `tap.m4a`, …). Any format your phone or Logic makes is fine.

- Speak slowly and softly, like a bedtime voice. There's no need to rush:
  the video waits for each line to finish.
- Leave a second of quiet before and after. The tool trims it and evens out
  the levels.
- If you change the wording, that's fine too: the picture follows the length of
  your line.

| # | File | Line | On screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `intro` | This is Deep Drift. A dark, quiet sea that sings you to sleep. | the sea appears |
| 2 | `tap` | Tap a sleepy jellyfish. Its note drifts away, and comes back, again and again. | one jellyfish is tapped |
| 3 | `weave` | Each one has its own loop, so the notes slowly weave together. | two more are tapped |
| 3b | `rest` | Tap a glowing one again to let it rest. | a glowing one is tapped again |
| 4 | `hold` | Hold one to hum its note, for as long as you like. | a jellyfish is held |
| 5 | `voices` | Pick a voice. Pad, Glass, Choir, or Low. Notes you played before keep their own sound. | Glass, then Choir |
| 6 | `depth` | Depth sinks everything lower, darker, and farther away. | Depth slider moves |
| 7 | `tide` | Tide sets how often the notes come back. | Tide slider moves |
| 8 | `tape` | Tape adds the wobble, warmth, and hiss of an old cassette. | Tape slider moves |
| 9 | `animate` | Or press Animate, and the tide moves on its own. | Animate turns on |
| 10 | `hum` | Hum adds a low drone underneath. | Hum turns on |
| 11 | `mood` | Change the mood up here. Every note still fits. | the mood changes |
| 12 | `sleep` | Set the sleep timer, and the sea fades away when it's time. | sleep timer set |
| 13 | `bye` | Tap a few lights, then close your eyes. Goodnight. | "goodnight" screen |
